Common questions

International schools, asked

Can you audit a school outside Hong Kong and the UAE?

Our current international footprint covers Hong Kong and the UAE. If your school is elsewhere, get in touch — we'll give you an honest answer on whether we can deliver to the same standard.

Do you work on-site or remotely?

Both. The core assessment is delivered on-site with your team, and we support remediation and follow-ups remotely after we leave.

Does an international school need KCSIE?

KCSIE itself is UK statutory guidance, but its principles — effective filtering, monitoring, and online safety for children — are exactly what international accreditation frameworks expect. We apply the same standards of care.

Is the audit useful before our accreditation visit?

Yes — that's often exactly when schools commission us. The report documents your position and gives you time to close gaps before inspectors arrive.
