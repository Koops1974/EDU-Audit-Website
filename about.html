About EduAudit

We exist for one reason: to give schools an honest, independent, standards-based view of their IT — so leadership teams can protect their pupils, satisfy inspectors, and make decisions with confidence.

Education-only practice Independent advice Cyber Smart Reseller
Who we are

Auditors who understand schools

EduAudit is an IT audit company built exclusively for education. We work with UK schools, academies, and trusts — and international schools in Hong Kong and the UAE. We don't sell IT. We only assess it.

◇

Education-only focus

Our entire practice is school IT. We speak the language of DSLs, governors, KCSIE, and Ofsted — and we know how IT genuinely operates in a school day.

◈

Standards-built platform

Our in-house auditing system maps every control to the requirements it satisfies — KCSIE, the DfE digital standards, UK GDPR, and international accreditation frameworks.

✦

Truly independent

No hardware. No software. No managed service. Nothing to sell you but an accurate picture and clear advice — which is exactly why schools trust the findings.

Our commitments

What you can expect from us

Clarity

Reports in plain English, structured so heads, governors, and business managers can act — not just IT teams.

Depth

We inspect real controls and real configurations. If something isn't in place, we say so — with the evidence to back every finding.

Standards

KCSIE, DfE digital technology standards, UK GDPR, and international frameworks are hardwired into how we audit.

Results

From compliance evidence for inspectors to Cyber Essentials and Plus certification — our support converts findings into outcomes.

Discretion

School data is sensitive. We handle your environment and your report with confidentiality and care, in line with UK GDPR practice.

Partnership

We stay with you beyond the report — supporting remediation, strategy, accreditation prep, and certification wherever you need us.
